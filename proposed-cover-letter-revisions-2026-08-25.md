## C2. Statement of the problem

### Current

> The cosmological arrow of time — the observed asymmetry between past and future on the largest scales — remains one of the deepest unresolved problems in theoretical physics. While the second law of thermodynamics accounts for time-asymmetry in macroscopic systems, it does not explain why the observable universe possesses a *uniform* and *global* sense of temporal direction, nor why the Big Bang was a low-entropy initial state. Any satisfying resolution must ultimately be grounded in a theory of quantum gravity.

### Proposed

> The existence of a coherent time orientation across the observable universe is logically prior to the thermodynamic question of why entropy increases. A quantum theory of geometry should explain how local future–past conventions become comparable over macroscopic distances. This manuscript proposes a gauge mechanism for that coherence; it does not claim to derive the low-entropy initial condition or the second law.

**Reason:** Define the paper's actual target before discussing the thermodynamic arrow.

## C3. Description of the proposal

### Current

> This paper presents a concrete microscopic mechanism for the emergence of a cosmological arrow of time within Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG). The central idea is to introduce a local Z₂ gauge field on spin networks, representing time-reversal symmetry acting independently at each vertex of the quantum geometry. Following Chen and Vishwanath's framework for gauging time-reversal symmetry on tensor network states, and exploiting the established spin-network/tensor-network correspondence, I show that the effective theory of this gauge field is a Z₂ lattice gauge theory admitting a confinement-deconfinement transition.

### Proposed

> This paper presents a concrete effective proposal within a restricted Loop Quantum Gravity setting. Binary tensor-network structure and the paired signs of the signed volume operator in the four-valent j=1/2 sector motivate a Z₂ link field for relative local orientation. I adopt the minimal Wilson action for that field and show that its confinement–deconfinement transition supplies a sharp mechanism for the emergence of long-distance orientation coherence.

**Reason:** Preserve the mechanism while avoiding the statement that the microscopic construction has already been derived.

## C4. Interpretation of the two phases

### Current

> - **Confined phase**: a pre-geometric "quantum gravitational foam" in which no coherent global arrow of time exists.
> - **Deconfined phase**: semiclassical spacetime with a uniform cosmological arrow of time, detected by a non-zero Wilson loop expectation value.

### Proposed

> - **Confined phase**: large flux fluctuations obstruct coherent comparison of orientation over long distances.
> - **Deconfined phase**: relative orientation can be transported coherently, diagnosed by the scaling of non-local gauge observables.

**Reason:** State what follows from the gauge theory without equating its phases directly with pre-geometry and observed semiclassical spacetime.

## C5. Elitzur's theorem

### Current

> This identification is fully consistent with Elitzur's theorem — the order parameter is the non-local Wilson loop, not a local field expectation value — resolving a potential objection that applies to earlier spontaneous-symmetry-breaking proposals for gauge theories.

### Proposed

> This identification respects Elitzur's theorem: the physical distinction is non-local Wilson-loop scaling, not a local gauge-charged expectation value.

**Reason:** Retain the exact gauge-theory point without making a broader claim about resolving earlier proposals.

## C6. CZX, numerics, and matter

### Current

> The deconfined phase is further identified with a symmetry-protected topological (SPT) phase of the CZX type, providing additional stability of the coherent time orientation against local perturbations. I establish a precise structural correspondence between 4-valent j=1/2 spin-network intertwiners and the CZX model, verified by an explicit computation showing that U_CZX acts as a Pauli-X gate on the intertwiner subspace. Finally, I conjecture — supported by three independent lines of argument — that the topologically protected surface excitations of this SPT phase give rise to fermionic matter degrees of freedom.

### Proposed

> The manuscript is explicit about its evidential boundary. CZX is a controlled reference construction, not an established microscopic LQG phase: an operator-level calculation shows that the literal CZX unitary does not preserve the full SU(2) singlet intertwiner subspace. The link field, Wilson coupling, semiclassical map, and cosmological trajectory remain targets for derivation from spin-foam dynamics. Bounded numerical controls reproduce the expected gauge-transition region, while a tested signed-volume dressing is retained as a negative result rather than presented as evidence.

**Reason:** Correct the operator claim, remove matter emergence from the submission pitch, and describe the actual numerical evidence.

## C7. Original contributions

### Current

1. Introduction of a local Z₂ gauge field on spin networks as the microscopic representation of time-reversal symmetry in LQG, with a derived Z₂ lattice gauge theory effective action.
2. Identification of the cosmological arrow of time with the confinement-deconfinement transition, with the Wilson loop as the order parameter, in full compliance with Elitzur's theorem.
3. A precise structural correspondence between four-valent j=1/2 LQG intertwiners and the CZX model, showing that the deconfined phase realizes CZX-type SPT order.
4. An explicit computation establishing the LQG–CZX isomorphism through the action of U_CZX.
5. A conjecture that surface excitations yield fermionic matter.

### Proposed

1. A clearly specified effective Z₂ orientation model motivated by the restricted spin-network sector.
2. A confinement–deconfinement mechanism for long-distance time-orientation coherence, formulated in gauge-invariant terms.
3. An operator-level obstruction that prevents dimensional matching with CZX from being mistaken for a microscopic embedding.
4. Reproducible numerical controls of the assumed gauge model, including an explicitly reported failed geometric diagnostic.
5. A falsifiable microscopic programme: derive, modify, or rule out the effective sector through spin-foam coarse graining.

**Reason:** Align the contribution list with the proposed evidential boundary.

## C8. Relation to the literature

### Current

> This work sits at the intersection of Loop Quantum Gravity, condensed matter (topological phases), and quantum information (tensor networks, QECC). It builds directly on the LQG–tensor network correspondence, the framework for gauging time-reversal symmetry on tensor networks, and the classification of SPT phases. To my knowledge, no prior work has used a confinement-deconfinement transition as the mechanism for the emergence of the cosmological arrow of time within LQG, nor established the LQG–CZX correspondence at the level of an explicit operator computation.

### Proposed

> This work sits at the intersection of Loop Quantum Gravity, topological phases, and tensor networks. It builds on the LQG–tensor-network correspondence, tensor-network gauging constructions, and standard Z₂ lattice gauge theory. Its distinctive proposal is to use deconfinement as the mechanism by which relative temporal orientation becomes coherent at cosmological scales, while treating the microscopic LQG realization as an open derivation problem.

**Reason:** Avoid claiming that the operator computation establishes an LQG–CZX correspondence when it instead reveals an obstruction.

## C9. Suitability for SciPost Physics

### Current

> This manuscript addresses a major open problem in quantum gravity using rigorous methods drawn from condensed matter and quantum information. The result is concrete and falsifiable in principle: it predicts a specific phase structure for the Z₂ lattice gauge theory on spin networks, with a Wilson loop order parameter, and specific surface anyonic statistics (all-fermion toric code) for the deconfined SPT phase. I believe this work meets SciPost Physics's criterion of opening a new direction in an established research area.

### Proposed

> The manuscript advances a definite, falsifiable mechanism rather than a completed fundamental theory. Its effective consequences are precise, its numerical controls are reproducible, and its missing microscopic bridges are stated as concrete tests. I believe this combination of conceptual reach and explicit falsifiability can open a useful new direction at the interface of quantum gravity and many-body theory.

**Reason:** Keep a confident publication case while removing unsupported predictions of surface statistics.
