## Overview

TimesArrow is a theoretical-physics manuscript proposing an effective $Z_2$ gauge mechanism for coherent cosmological time orientation in a restricted spin-network setting.

## Publication Audience

- Quantum-gravity researchers assessing the LQG and spin-foam assumptions.
- Condensed-matter and quantum-information researchers assessing gauge and SPT language.

## Current Product Decision

Path A is the publication strategy. The paper will study consequences of an assumed effective $Z_2$ sector and will not present the microscopic CZX/LQG embedding as complete. Gauge-transition numerics are implementation controls, not evidence that quantum geometry produces an arrow of time.
