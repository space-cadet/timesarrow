## 🚨 CRITICAL FINDING: K₄ is NOT compatible with cluster state framework

### Evidence from Literature Survey (Track 2 — COMPLETE)

#### 1. Jia 2024 (JHEP09(2024)147) — **FUNDAMENTAL OBSTRUCTION**

**Finding:** Cluster graphs **MUST be bipartite** (Definition 2, p. 17):
> "A graph K(V,E) is called a cluster graph if (i) it is a bipartite graph..."

**Finding:** Even vertices with >2 bonds break commutativity (Proposition 6, p. 21):
> "if the even vertex v_e has more than two bonds (|N_E(v_e)| > 2), the commutativity cannot be reached in general."

**Implication:**
- **K₄** (complete graph on 4 vertices) contains triangles → **NOT bipartite** ❌
- **C₄** (cycle of length 4/ring) **IS bipartite** → VALID cluster graph ✅
- K₄ cannot support standard CSS-type cluster state construction
- Edge entangler operators for K4 connectivity do NOT commute

**Conclusion:** K₄ and C₄ are **NOT equivalent** under any local operation or gauging.

#### 2. Inamura 2021 (JHEP03(2022)036) — No Obstruction

- Commuting projector framework is **algebraic/geometry-agnostic**
- Works for any lattice where local Hilbert space structure is respected
- No explicit discussion of bipartite vs non-bipartite lattices
- **Verdict:** No obstruction from this paper, but also no support for K₄ specifically

#### 3. Inamura & Ohyama 2026 (arXiv:2601.08615) — No Direct Relevance

- Focuses on 2+1D generalized cluster states via **gauging construction**
- Does NOT address K₄ vs C₄ or graph connectivity
- Different approach that doesn't rely on bipartite graph structure
- **Verdict:** No direct relevance to K₄ question
