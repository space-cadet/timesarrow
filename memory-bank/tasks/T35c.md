## Context

Following the July 21–22 discussions with Terra and Deepak, three candidate models were identified for the CZX construction on the diamond lattice:

1. **Edge-qubit** (T35b old): ❌ Obstructed at L=3
2. **Intertwiner coarse-site** (Terra's spec): 🔄 Spec written, Gate 0 open
3. **Face-qubit** (K₄ connectivity): ❓ This task

The face-qubit model places qubits on tetrahedron faces (4 per vertex) with intrinsic K₄ connectivity. Unlike the C₄ ring, the K₄ operator is all-to-all. The key open question is whether this can be realized as a valid SPT on the diamond lattice, given the geometric frustration from 6 hexagons sharing 4 qubits at each vertex.

## Key Question

**Does the K₄ face-qubit model survive geometric frustration?**

- If **yes**: It may be a valid (non-commuting) SPT, possibly in the same phase as C₄.
- If **no**: The obstruction is geometric — face-qubits cannot support commuting projectors on the diamond lattice.

## Three Investigation Tracks

### Track 1: Numerical Test (Empirical)

Implement the frustrated Hamiltonian on small clusters:

```
H = Σ_p (I - P_p)   # non-commuting plaquette projectors
```

**Targets:**
- Single tetrahedron (4 qubits): Verify K₄ algebra, eigenvalues, GHZ invariance
- Two tetrahedra (8 qubits): Check for gap, ground state structure
- Small diamond cluster (L=2, L=3): Lanczos or ED

**Checks:**
- Ground state energy
- Gap (if any)
- Ground state degeneracy
- Symmetry properties

### Track 2: Literature Search

**Search terms:**
- "SPT diamond lattice"
- "tetrahedral symmetry protected topological"
- "K4 CZ gate" / "complete graph CZ"
- "non-commuting projector Hamiltonian SPT"
- "face qubit lattice gauge theory"

**Key questions:**
- Has anyone constructed an SPT on the diamond lattice?
- Are there known obstructions for SPTs on bipartite lattices with face degrees of freedom?
- Is K₄ connectivity studied in quantum information (beyond standard CZ)?

### Track 3: Alternative Geometries

Test K₄ on lattices without frustration:

| Lattice | Plaquette | Qubits/site | Frustration? |
|---------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Square** | 4-cycle | 4 | ✅ None |
| **Cubic** | 4-cycle (face) | 4 | ✅ None |
| **Diamond** | 6-cycle (hexagon) | 4 | ❌ Yes — 6 plaquettes share 4 qubits |

If K₄ works on square/cubic but not diamond, the obstruction is **geometric**, not algebraic.

## Risks

- **Numerical cost**: Frustrated Hamiltonians may have dense spectra; Lanczos may struggle.
- **Negative result**: The face-qubit model may simply not work on the diamond lattice.
- **Ambiguity**: Even if a gap exists, proving SPT equivalence to C₄ requires boundary anomaly calculation.

## Notes

- The K₄ operator is **not** the same as the standard CZ ring. It has 6 CZ factors vs 4.
- Both stabilize GHZ, but act differently on excited states.
- The geometric frustration problem is specific to the **face-qubit placement**. Vertex-qubit or coarse-site models avoid it.
- Deepak's original Z₂ gauge theory numerics (T20/T31/T33) are **unaffected** — they used edge-qubits, not face-qubits.
